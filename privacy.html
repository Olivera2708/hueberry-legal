Hueberry Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 14, 2026

This policy explains how the Hueberry mobile game (the "App"), made by cubeorcoding ("we", "us"), handles information. It applies to the App on Android and iOS.

In short: Hueberry does not collect any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we do not run any server that receives your data. Your game progress stays on your device. Purchases are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store.

Information stored on your device

The App saves the following only on your device, so the game works and remembers your progress:

  • Game progress: levels, stars, scores, daily challenge results, streaks, statistics and achievements.
  • Your coin balance and whether you own Hueberry Premium.
  • Settings such as language, theme, sound and reminder preferences.

We never receive this information. It is deleted when you uninstall the App or use "Reset progress" in the settings (Premium is kept and can be restored from the store).

Reminders and widgets

If you turn on daily reminders, the App schedules notifications on your device. No notification server is involved and no notification token is sent to us. Home screen widgets read the same on-device game data.

Sharing

When you choose to share a result, the App creates an image on your device and opens your system share menu. You decide where it goes. We do not receive it.

Advertising and tracking

The App does not show ads and does not include any advertising, analytics or tracking software. It does not access your device's advertising identifier and does not track you across other apps or websites.

In-app purchases

Purchases (Hueberry Premium and coin packs) are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see your name, payment card or billing details. The App only receives a purchase confirmation from the store on your device, so it can unlock Premium or add coins.

Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
Apple's privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

Your choices

  • Your rights: depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, or to object to its use. Because we do not collect or hold any of your data, there is nothing for us to access or delete, but you can always contact us with questions.
  • Delete everything: uninstalling the App deletes all data it stored on your device.

Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Security

Game data stays on your device and is protected by your device's own security. Purchase data is handled by Google and Apple under their own policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when the App gets new features. If a future version adds ads or any data collection, this policy will be updated before that version is released. The effective date above shows the latest version.
