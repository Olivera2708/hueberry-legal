Hueberry Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 12, 2026

This policy explains how the Hueberry mobile game (the "App"), made by cubeorcoding ("we", "us"), handles information. It applies to the App on Android and iOS.

In short: Hueberry has no accounts and we do not run any server that receives your data. Your game progress stays on your device. Ads are provided through CAS.AI (Clever Ads Solutions) and its advertising partners, and purchases are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. Those companies collect some data as described below.

Information stored on your device

The App saves the following only on your device, so the game works and remembers your progress:

  • Game progress: levels, stars, scores, daily challenge results, streaks, statistics and achievements.
  • Your coin balance and whether you own Hueberry Premium.
  • Settings such as language, theme, sound and reminder preferences.

We never receive this information. It is deleted when you uninstall the App or use "Reset progress" in the settings (Premium is kept and can be restored from the store).

Reminders and widgets

If you turn on daily reminders, the App schedules notifications on your device. No notification server is involved and no notification token is sent to us. Home screen widgets read the same on-device game data.

Sharing

When you choose to share a result, the App creates an image on your device and opens your system share menu. You decide where it goes. We do not receive it.

Advertising (CAS.AI)

The free version of the App shows ads through CAS.AI (Clever Ads Solutions, operated by CLEAR INVEST LTD), an ad mediation platform. CAS.AI selects ads from third-party ad networks (its mediation partners), and the partners serving ads in the App may change over time. To show and measure ads, CAS.AI and these partners may automatically collect:

  • Your device's advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID on Android; on iOS the IDFA only if you allow tracking).
  • Device and network information, such as device model, operating system version, language, and IP address (from which an approximate location can be derived).
  • Information about the ads you see and interact with, such as views and clicks.

This data is used to deliver ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure ad performance, prevent fraud and, where you have agreed, to show personalized ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the EU, UK and some US states), the App asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, and on iOS it asks for permission before tracking.

CAS.AI's privacy policy: https://cas.ai/privacy-policy/. Each mediation partner processes data under its own privacy policy.

Hueberry Premium removes all ads.

In-app purchases

Purchases (Hueberry Premium and coin packs) are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see your name, payment card or billing details. The App only receives a purchase confirmation from the store on your device, so it can unlock Premium or add coins.

Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
Apple's privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

Your choices

  • Personalized ads: on Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads). On iOS you can turn off tracking in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Your rights: depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, or to object to its use. Because we do not hold your data, requests about advertising data are best sent to CAS.AI through its privacy policy. You can also contact us and we will help.
  • Delete everything: uninstalling the App deletes all data it stored on your device.

Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used the App in a way that led to data being collected, contact us.

Security

Game data stays on your device and is protected by your device's own security. Data collected by CAS.AI and its partners, Google and Apple is protected under their own policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when the App gets new features. The effective date above shows the latest version. Significant changes will be noted in the App's store listing.
