Hueberry Support

Help for Hueberry, the color game where one color is wrong.

Need help or found a bug? Email cubeorcoding@gmail.com and we'll get back to you, usually within a few days. Please mention your device model and the app version (shown at the bottom of Settings).

How do I play?

Every picture has one wrong color, marked with a dashed outline. Move the hue, saturation and brightness sliders until it looks right, then tap Lock in. You get a score from 0 to 100 for how close you were: 75 or more earns one star, 88 two stars and 95 three stars.

I bought Premium on a new device. How do I get it back?

Open Settings and tap Restore purchase. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you used to buy it. Premium is free to restore on all your devices.

I bought coins but didn't receive them

Coins are added as soon as the store confirms the payment. If a purchase is pending (for example waiting for approval), the coins arrive when it completes. If they still don't show up, close and reopen the app. If that doesn't help, email us with the date of the purchase.

Where is my progress saved? Can I move it to another device?

Hueberry has no accounts, so your levels, stars, streaks and stats are saved only on your device. They stay when you update the app, but are removed if you delete it, and they can't be moved to another device. Premium can always be restored (see above).

Why didn't my daily reminder show up?

Turn on Daily reminder in Settings and allow notifications for Hueberry in your device's settings. The reminder is skipped on days you've already played the daily challenge.

How do I add the widget?

Touch and hold an empty area of your home screen, tap the add (+) button, search for Hueberry and choose a widget size. It shows your daily streak.

How does party mode work?

Party mode is for 3 or more players on one phone or tablet. One player secretly picks a color and gives a one-word clue, everyone else tries to recreate it, then a second clue and guess follow. Pass the device around and keep the screen hidden from the others when it's not your turn.
